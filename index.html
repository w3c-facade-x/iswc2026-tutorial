Overview

A growing community of researchers, practitioners, and tool builders has coalesced around Façade-X, a design philosophy and metamodel for querying heterogeneous data formats — CSV, JSON, XML, spreadsheets, and more — as knowledge graphs, using SPARQL as the sole integration language.

This tutorial brings that community to ISWC, disseminating the methods, technologies, and philosophy behind the approach. SPARQL Anything, the reference open-source implementation, serves as the primary teaching tool, while the tutorial also showcases independent implementations emerging in commercial platforms, reflecting the maturity of the approach. The W3C Façade-X Community Group, active since late 2025, provides the standardisation backdrop.

Why Façade-X?

Integrating heterogeneous data formats remains one of the most persistent practical obstacles in knowledge graph construction. Conventional approaches rely on dedicated mapping languages and separate toolchains, adding complexity that often falls to domain experts rather than software engineers. Façade-X takes a different route: by exposing any format as a knowledge graph queryable with SPARQL alone, it removes the need for a second mapping language and keeps the entire workflow within familiar Semantic Web standards.

Requirements & audience

For participants

  • Bring a laptop with SPARQL Anything pre-downloaded, or PySPARQL Anything installed in a Python environment or Jupyter notebook.
  • Stable Wi-Fi is essential for the live querying exercises. As a contingency, a prepared Google Colab notebook will be available if local installations are unavailable.

Who it's for

The tutorial is application- and tool-focused, pitched at an intermediate level and targeted at knowledge graph engineers, data integration practitioners, Semantic Web researchers, and educators. Attendees are expected to have basic familiarity with SPARQL and at least one non-RDF data format (CSV, JSON, or XML). No prior knowledge of Façade-X or SPARQL Anything is assumed.
